Stem glyphs

Same 5×5 state machine as the spotting board, re-rendered with the brand contour's construction rule instead of a dot matrix.

1 · The rule, checked against the logo

Left: the contour SVG from Figma. Right: a 4×2 module grid with four modules "on", drawn by the rule. An isolated module is a circle; orthogonal neighbours bridge into a capsule; and wherever an off module sits between two lit neighbours (an inside corner, or a diagonal step) its inscribed circle is cut away as the fillet. Nothing else — the diagonal join is the whole signature.

2 · Every state, side by side

Left pair is the current dot matrix (16 px and 40 px). Right pair is the stem rendering at the same sizes.
